Welcome to Straighten Up

Women's Health

This isn't medical advice. Straighten Up: Women's Health is a personal tracking tool, not a diagnosis, treatment plan, or substitute for care from a doctor or qualified provider. Always talk to your physician or a functional-medicine provider before starting, stopping, or changing any supplement, medication, or HRT regimen — especially given possible interactions with medications you may already take.
Your data stays on your device. Everything you log — symptoms, cycle dates, labs, HRT — is stored privately in your phone or browser's own storage. It is never uploaded to a server or shared with us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data will permanently delete it, so use the Export tab regularly if you'd like a backup or a copy for your doctor.
